# ISO 29148-shaped RE (working draft)

This repo adopts the **shape** of ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 (Ed2; Ed3 DIS has no
force until published): StRS → SRS → traceability; quality characteristics
(necessary, singular, unambiguous, verifiable, …); separate OpsCon.

**Working draft acknowledgment:** requirement texts and QAS measures may carry
`Draft` / `MEASURE-TBD` until Spikes and stakeholder Accept. Draft ≠ optional
standards — the *form* is mandatory; the *values* mature.

Live artifacts: `docs/requirements/*`, `docs/constraints/constraints.md`.
Deep method: `research/atam-formal/`, `research/adversarial/`.
