# ISO 29148-shaped requirements engineering (working draft)

Adopt the **shape** of ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 (Ed2; Ed3 DIS has no force until
published): Stakeholder Requirements Specification → Software Requirements
Specification → traceability; quality characteristics (necessary, singular,
unambiguous, verifiable, …); separate OpsCon.

**Working draft:** requirement texts and Quality Attribute Scenario measures may
carry `Draft` / `MEASURE-TBD` until Spikes and stakeholder Accept. Draft ≠
optional form — the *form* is mandatory; the *values* mature. Skipping the form
fails the no-code gate.

Live artifacts: `docs/requirements/*`, `docs/constraints/constraints.md`.  
Deep method: `research/atam-formal/`, `research/adversarial/`.
